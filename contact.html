Common questions

The things people write in about most often.

What can I use right now?

Reading for pleasure is open today: the family shelf, the resources, the reading log and the wishlists are all live. The services page sets out everything a family account includes.

Who sees my family’s details?

Only you, and the family members you invite to your shelf. Children’s profiles are never public, nothing is shared with advertisers, and you can delete your account at any time.

How do I suggest a book or resource?

Signed-in parents can use the suggestion board inside Reading for pleasure. Otherwise, use the form above and pick “Suggesting a book or resource”. A moderator checks every suggestion before it is shared — see how we check what is shared.

Can my child have their own account?

No. Parents Go To is a website for parents. Children can use parts of a project, such as bedtime mode, only through a parent’s account and with the parent in charge.

Something on the site is wrong or unsuitable. What now?

Tell us through the form above. The report goes to a moderator, who can take the resource or post down while they look into it.

I cannot use part of this site.

Please tell us, and say which page and what happened. Accessibility reports go to the top of the queue. If you are already signed in to Reading for pleasure, the accessibility statement in that app has a direct route as well.
