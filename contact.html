Common questions

The things people write in about most often.

What can I use right now?

Reading for pleasure is open today: the family shelf, the resources, the reading log and the wishlists are all live. The services page sets out everything a family account includes.

Who sees my family’s details?

Only the provider you chose to book with, and only what they need to run the session. Nothing is shared with advertisers, and you can delete your profile at any time.

How do I list my service?

Use the form above and pick “Listing my own service”. We will ask for your insurance certificate, staff clearances and an address before the listing goes live — see how we check providers.

Something on a listing is wrong. What now?

Tell us through the form above. The report goes to a moderator rather than to the provider, and we will pause the listing while we look into it.

I cannot use part of this site.

Please tell us, and say which page and what happened. Accessibility reports go to the top of the queue. If you are already signed in to Reading for pleasure, the accessibility statement in that app has a direct route as well.
