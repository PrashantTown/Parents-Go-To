About Parents Go To

One trusted place for parents to find, save and share information that suits their children, starting with reading.

Why we built it

There is a huge amount of information online about children’s reading and development, but it is scattered across many websites. AI-generated content and social media influencers make it harder still to know which recommendations to trust. Parents spend a lot of time searching and struggle to judge whether something is suitable or reliable.

Parents Go To brings trusted information together in one place. You add your children once, and the site shows what suits each of them by age and by interest. Each project appears as a tile on the home page. Reading for pleasure is the first, Four Voices is next, and areas such as health and hobbies can be added later.

How we look after your information

We only collect what the site needs to work. Parents hold the account — children do not have their own logins, and anything they use opens through a parent account with the parent in charge. We do not share your details with advertisers, and you can delete your account at any time.

At a glance

  • One parent account for the whole family
  • Matched to each child’s age and interests
  • Books and resources reviewed before they are shared
  • Parent posts moderated before they appear
  • Wishlists you can share with relatives
  • Your account can be deleted at any time

The team

Her work focuses on strengthening young children’s language, literacy and learning through evidence-informed practice. Her expertise includes early language development, caregiver–child communication, dialogic interaction, shared storybook reading and quality interactions.

  • LC

    Dr Laine Chilman

    Dr Laine Chilman is a full-time senior lecturer in Occupational Therapy. She is committed to research that enhances children’s lives by supporting parents. She investigates occupational therapy and parenting interventions that promote child development and wellbeing, particularly in terms of child behaviour, play, picky eating, and the vital support systems necessary for optimal development. Specifically, her interest in understanding and mitigating the impact of picky eating on families fuelled her to uncover innovative solutions for healthcare practices, including supporting parents through occupational performance coaching. Laine’s work emphasises her dedication to co-design with First Nation communities and a lived experience perspective, which enriches the research process.

Four Voices

Four Voices Around the Table

Four Voices Around the Table invites families to share ideas, resources and conversations around storybooks, everyday experiences and children’s language development. It provides a welcoming space for parents to participate, connect, share experiences and learn together through early language resources and activities.

How we check what is shared

Nothing appears until it has been reviewed.

  1. Books and resources are curated

    Our team chooses the books and outside resources that appear, and checks each one before it is published.

  2. Parent posts are moderated

    Suggestions, reviews and message board posts are read by a moderator before other parents can see them.

  3. Matched by age and interest

    Each item is tagged with the ages and categories it suits, so the filters show what fits your child.

  4. Kept up to date

    Moderators can edit, hide or remove anything that is out of date or no longer suitable.

If something looks wrong or unsuitable, tell us. A report goes to a moderator, who can take it down while they look into it. Report a problem.
