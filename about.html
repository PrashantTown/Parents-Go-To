About Parents Go To

We are trying to fix one specific problem: good services for families exist, and parents cannot find them.

Why we built it

Most families do not struggle to attend a session. They struggle to find out it exists. Council pages, school newsletters and community noticeboards rarely talk to each other, so good programmes sit half empty while parents are still looking for them.

Parents Go To puts those services in one place. You set your family up once, and after that you are comparing like with like: same filters, and the same way of showing what age a session actually suits.

What we will not do

We do not share your details with advertisers, and we do not ask for your email address before showing you what a session involves. Everything a family needs to decide is on the listing.

At a glance

  • One profile for the whole family
  • Providers checked before listing
  • Libraries and community groups can list sessions
  • Your profile can be deleted at any time

It provides a welcoming space for parents to participate, connect, share experiences and learn together through early language resources and activities.

How we check providers

Four steps, before a listing appears and once a year after.

  1. A real, findable organisation

    We confirm the business or group is registered, and that the address on the listing is somewhere sessions actually run.

  2. Insurance in force

    We sight a current public liability certificate, and we record the date it expires so the listing can be paused if it lapses.

  3. Staff clearances

    Anyone working unsupervised with children holds a current working-with-children check. We record the clearance type and the date it was verified.

  4. The listing matches the session

    We check that ages, times and what to bring describe the session as it is really run — not as it was two years ago.

If something is wrong with a listing, tell us. A report goes to a moderator, not to the provider, and we will take a listing down while we look into it. Report a listing.
