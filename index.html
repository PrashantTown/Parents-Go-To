Why parents use it

The search is the hard part.

Most families do not struggle to attend a session. They struggle to find out it exists. Council pages, school newsletters and community noticeboards rarely talk to each other, so good programmes stay half empty while parents look for them.

Your details stay yours

We share only what a provider needs to run the session, and only with the provider you picked. Nothing is shared with advertisers, and you can delete your profile at any time.

Built for the whole family

One profile can hold several children of different ages. Search results show which of your children a session actually suits, so you are not doing the maths every time.

Hear when a service opens

One short email when a new category goes live in your area. Nothing else.
