Why parents use it

Too much information, not enough trust.

Advice on children’s reading and development is spread across many websites, and AI-generated content and social media make it harder to tell what is reliable. Parents spend hours searching and still are not sure what suits their child. Parents Go To brings trusted information together in one place, sorted by age and interest.

Your details stay yours

We only ask for what the site needs to work. Your children’s profiles are seen only by you and the family members you invite. Nothing is shared with advertisers, and you can delete your account at any time.

Built for the whole family

One parent account holds several children of different ages. Children do not have their own logins — anything they use opens through your account, with you in charge. Grandparents and relatives can view a wishlist you share with them.

Checked before it is shared

Books and resources are chosen by our team, and posts from other parents are read by a moderator before anyone else can see them.

Hear when a new project opens

One short email when a new project or category goes live. Nothing else.
